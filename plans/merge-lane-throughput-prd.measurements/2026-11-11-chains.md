### Train members' superseded rows

NOT script output. This probe takes the set of all `train_merged` members up to the read bound
and counts their rows inside each window. Its row counts sit inside the script's
`merge_attempt` superseded outcome and its `merge_finalized` states.

```sql
WITH w(name, lo, hi) AS (VALUES
 ('BASELINE','2026-08-20T16:10:00+00:00','2026-09-03T16:10:00+00:00'),
 ('E_AFTER','2026-10-01T05:01:29+00:00','2026-10-15T05:01:29+00:00'),
 ('H_BEFORE','2026-10-13T22:59:35.909350+00:00','2026-10-27T22:59:35.909350+00:00'),
 ('H_AFTER','2026-10-27T22:59:35.909350+00:00','2026-11-10T22:59:35.909350+00:00')),
m AS (SELECT CAST(j.value AS TEXT) AS task_id, t.timestamp AS train_ts
      FROM events t, json_each(t.data,'$.member_task_ids') j
      WHERE t.event_type = 'train_merged' AND t.timestamp < '2026-11-11T20:30:00+00:00')
SELECT w.name,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM events f WHERE f.event_type = 'merge_finalized' AND f.timestamp >= w.lo AND f.timestamp < w.hi
     AND CAST(f.task_id AS TEXT) IN (SELECT task_id FROM m)) AS finalized_rows_of_members,
  count(f.id) AS finalized_superseded_of_members,
  count(DISTINCT f.task_id) AS distinct_members,
  sum(f.timestamp < m.train_ts AND m.train_ts < w.hi) AS before_own_train_in_window,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM events a WHERE a.event_type = 'merge_attempt' AND json_extract(a.data,'$.outcome') = 'superseded'
     AND a.timestamp >= w.lo AND a.timestamp < w.hi AND CAST(a.task_id AS TEXT) IN (SELECT task_id FROM m)) AS attempt_superseded_of_members
FROM w LEFT JOIN events f ON f.event_type = 'merge_finalized' AND json_extract(f.data,'$.state') = 'superseded'
     AND f.timestamp >= w.lo AND f.timestamp < w.hi
JOIN m ON m.task_id = CAST(f.task_id AS TEXT)
GROUP BY w.name ORDER BY w.lo;
```

| window | members' `merge_finalized` rows, of the script's total | members' `merge_finalized` superseded rows, of the script's superseded | distinct members | of those rows, written before their own train landed in the same window | members' `merge_attempt` superseded rows, of the script's superseded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BASELINE | 2 of 369 | 2 of 49 | 2 | 2 | 2 of 3 |
| E_AFTER | 15 of 633 | 15 of 43 | 15 | 15 | 13 of 29 |
| H_BEFORE | 80 of 781 | 78 of 149 | 74 | 78 | 78 of 149 |
| H_AFTER | 78 of 666 | 78 of 149 | 78 | 78 | 78 of 149 |

The "of" totals are the script's own cells for the same window. The distinct-member counts equal
each window's train-member count (Train landings, above), so every member of a train that landed
in a window has a superseded row in it. H_BEFORE's other 2 member rows are `blocked`. For train
545962 (members 6354, 5551 and 6321) the superseded rows are ids 544702–544704 at
2026-11-01T02:09:20Z, and the train landed at 07:56:21Z.
