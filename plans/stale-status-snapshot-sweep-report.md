## Triage (by eye)

Read by eye on 2026-10-10 over the unselected samples listed above (the first 25 by edge uuid).

Most are NOT current-status snapshots. They are historical or narrative facts whose status word describes something else or a past state: 'was blocked for five days', 'is now done, having been pending', 'found no pending escalations', 'Active/Pending/Done checkboxes', 'applied a third status value blocked'. Retiring them would be over-selection.

The genuine snapshots the shipped rules miss fall into four lexical classes. Each sits deliberately outside the closed-class copula/article anchoring:

1. Relative clause: 'tracked by task 5944, which is pending'; 'task 3708 (...), which is still pending'.
2. Attributive pre-modifier or predicate nominal: 'Blocked task 4761 is based on ...'; 'Task 2238 is the high-priority pending follow-up'.
3. Gerund or participle: 'due to being pending with no agent'.
4. Secondary or coordinated predicate: 'is filed and pending'; 'is filed pending'; 'is tracked as pending'.

All four are under-selection, the fail-safe direction. Such an edge stays valid until the periodic sweep, Stage 2 or a later rewrite retires it. None is a precision hazard. No class is widened by task 4851: each would need its own precision pass against the non-snapshot shapes above.
