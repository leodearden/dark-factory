## CAVEATS
- MCP submission is not recorded anywhere in runs.db: merge_queued data is only {branch, queue_depth}, and merge_attempt/merge_finalized carry no submitter field. 'Non-workflow member' is inferred from missing workflow-park evidence (a phase_enter 'merge-deferred' after the superseded row). Four early-July members (2487, 2737, 2763, 2895) have no superseded merge_finalized row and were classed by their train_member_deferred status instead.
- train_coalesced.exclusions is emitted only when a train forms. Passes that leave fewer than 2 eligible candidates are log-only ('Coalesce near-train suppressed'). The journald supplement covers only 2026-10-10 onward for exclusions and 2026-10-14 onward for suppressed passes. I could not establish how many MCP singles existed before 10-14, because the pre-4953 era had no exclusion.
- Cause for never-started trains before 2026-10-07 (9 trains) is inferred from run boundaries (a new run started 10 min to 3.5 h after formation), because journald coverage for the 'dissolving it' / 'starting atomic merge' lines starts at 2026-10-07 and the dissolve-on-halt code (task 5371) merged 2026-10-06. Whether each was dissolved by an admission halt or killed unstarted cannot be separated; I labelled them NS. The 3 started-no-terminal trains are likewise inferred to be restart casualties, not proven.
- Task 7258-3b3e04b3 has no journal line at all; I infer it was lost in the old process during the 10-22 drain (its members' age_anchor_reset pending_since is 08:54Z, after the 08:23Z restart, consistent with the abandoned-member sweep).
- The before/after comparison is confounded: task 5371 (admission-halt dissolution, 10-06), task 5352 (durable one-strike, 09-25), task 4737 (abandoned-member sweep, 10-15), and changes to the gate/CAS loop landed in the same period. The drop in cas_failed derails after 09-26 and the rise in dissolutions from 10-11 are not attributable to 4953 alone. Timing: cutover by commit (2026-10-13T21:48Z) and by deploy (2026-10-14T03:30Z) gives identical counts since no train formed in between.
- Verify-hours are host-verify seconds summed from merge_verify rows attributed to a train by (tip task, timestamp window). Solo verifies pipeline speculatively, so verify-hours are not lane wall-hours. The solo baseline is the weekly median over all passed non-train merge_verify rows, which swings from about 1 min (June) to 61 min (w/c 09-07); the saving figure is sensitive to it. Derailed trains' member re-verifies as singles are not counted as waste. A train timed out at 7200 s (6738-d2fe7df6, 154 min) is included in the waste.
- Post-derail fates are right-censored: after-4953 derailed members have had at most about 3 days. 'No later landing' is not 'stranded': 33 of the 83 no-landing members are status done in tasks.db via a path with no lane event (hand merge, found_on_main, or an earlier event), and 40 are pending, possibly awaiting dispatch or superseded work. tasks.db was read live at about 2026-10-26 18:00Z. Lags for never-started trains are anchored at formation, so they include the pre-dissolution wait.
- The solo-vs-train member latency comparison is selection-biased (trains form from an already-waiting backlog and wait in the lane buffer tail), so it does not show that trains slow an equivalent member.
- The 'union verify failed' tag is the lane's interaction-candidate label, not proof of cross-member interaction: one of 22 is a 7200 s infra timeout, and 13 of 22 name ratchet or baseline-style tests, which may be ratchet-ledger conflicts or flakes. I did not adjudicate individual verify logs.
- Declared (non-coalesce) trains produce no train_* events in this store's window; all 220 trains are coalesce trains. The store holds train events only from 2026-07-18; coalescing before that is invisible. merge_finalized exists from 2026-06-09 and the `merge_coalesced` events (20 rows, to 2026-09-18) are a different feature (attach to an in-flight request), not trains.
- Per-train rows list members by task id in train order (tip last). Scripts used are in /tmp/e2 (load.py, m2.py, main.py, fates2.py, q4b.py, final_q2q3.py, jr.py, near.py); they are scratch files, not committed. I did not modify any repo file and ran no git stash.
